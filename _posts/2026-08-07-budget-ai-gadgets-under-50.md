Not every AI gadget needs to be a $200+ centerpiece purchase. A lot of genuinely useful "smart"
functionality has trickled down into cheap, everyday objects — plugs, bottles, clips — that most
people wouldn't think to call AI gadgets at all. Here are five picks that stay firmly in
budget territory, roughly $50 or under at time of writing (some closer to $10-15).

## 1. Smart Plug With Energy Monitoring

A WiFi smart plug that doesn't just turn things on and off remotely — it tracks how much power
whatever's plugged into it is actually using over time, and can flag unusual consumption patterns
(like a device drawing power it shouldn't be, hours after you thought you turned it off).

**Why it's interesting:** This is the cheapest, lowest-effort way to actually see where electricity
use is going in a home, without an electrician or a whole-home monitoring system.

**Who it's for:** Anyone curious what's actually driving their electricity bill, or who wants basic
"turn this off automatically" scheduling without committing to a bigger smart home setup.

## 3. Smart Water Bottle With Drink Reminders

drink based on time elapsed rather than a fixed schedule — some models add UV self-cleaning too.

**Why it's interesting:** The "reminder" part only works if it's actually attached to something you
carry anyway. Building it into the bottle itself is a much stickier habit prompt than a phone
notification you'll swipe away.

**Who it's for:** Anyone who's tried a hydration-tracking app and stopped opening it within a week —
