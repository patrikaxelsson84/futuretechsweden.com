## Format reminder for future posts
- 5-item listicle, ~600-900 words, same structure as posts 1-2: intro → per-item (what/why
  interesting/who it's for/CTA button) → closing "where to start" section → price-changes disclaimer.
- Never claim first-hand testing/results we haven't actually done — keep language to
  research-based "why it's interesting" framing until real hands-on reviews exist.
- Each new post should link back to at least one earlier post once there are 3+ live (internal
  linking helps SEO and keeps readers on site longer).
