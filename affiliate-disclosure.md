# Affiliate Disclosure

FutureTech Sweden is reader-supported. When you buy a product through some of the links on this
site, we may earn a commission from the retailer — including but not limited to AliExpress and
Amazon — at no extra cost to you.

## How this works

We participate in affiliate programs, including the AliExpress Affiliate Program and Amazon
Associates. Links marked as affiliate links (usually a "Check the current price" button or similar
call-to-action) go through a tracking link. If you make a purchase after clicking one, the retailer
pays us a small commission for referring you.

## Does this affect the price I pay?

No. The commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket. You pay the same price
whether you click our link or go to the store directly.

## Does this affect what we recommend?

No. We only feature products we've researched and would genuinely recommend, whether or not an
affiliate link is attached. Commission potential is never the reason a product is included or
excluded from an article.
